A monthly check on the field's journals

Each month I check every journal indexed in Pinakes against public records: whether its domain and HTTPS certificate are current, whether an archive holds a copy of it, and whether the registries that libraries and search engines read can find it. The table has one row per journal, and each journal's page shows the record behind every result.

The results describe infrastructure, not scholarship. For each check, the methods page says where the data comes from and what an editor can do about a result.
